Permanent Action

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once processed, your GeoGuardMe profile, premium safety passes, and subscription access will be permanently removed. To help keep other travelers safe, any incident reports you have submitted will remain on the map but will be completely anonymized. You will lose all access to Guard Mode and real-time audio alerts immediately.

According to Google Play Store and Apple App Store policies, you have the right to request the deletion of your account and all personal data associated with it.
